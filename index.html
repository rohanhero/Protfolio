About Me

I'm a frontend developer based in Hetauda, Nepal, and a Bachelor of Computer Application graduate from Hetauda City College under Tribhuvan University. I'm drawn to the parts of tech that connect things interfaces to people, machines to networks, and data to decisions.

I've shipped Welcome Flooring & Furnishing, a live e-commerce site for a home furnishings brand built solo, end to end, and in production today. More recently I built NovaStore, a full-stack e-commerce platform pairing a Django REST API with a React storefront and a complete admin panel. Alongside these, I built Food4All & EcoGuard, a food-sharing platform and a smart municipality management platform, and spent time as a Network Specialist intern at Worldlink Communication getting hands-on with the infrastructure side of the internet. In parallel, I've followed the Nepal Stock Exchange as a technical analyst since 2023, a habit that's sharpened how I read patterns, on a chart or in a codebase.

Whatever I'm working on, the goal is the same: clean, purposeful execution over noise.
